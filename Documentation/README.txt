This site was concieved from the Persona games.

These games have a unique calendar system. You have to balance your social life with visits to the dungeon where you fight.
One example of this is how you manage relationships, known as social links or confidants depending on the game.
Social links may only be available on different days, so you have to keep track of when they are available to manage leveling and maintenance.
Another example is sales. There can be multiple shops in these games that may only run sales on certain days.
Finally, you need to be able to raise your social stats. These help you gain access to important social links or special events.
These social stat events only happen on certain days, such as games being available at an arcade or movies shown at the theatre.

This site will enable the user to track social links, sales, and stat events by inputting information into form(s) and being able to toggle through the days
of the week. It will also house a database of all social links or confidants based on which game you are playing, and have information such as
if they are romancable, where they can be found, and of course what days they are available.

Another file will be development notes, which will document all thoughts and progress.
